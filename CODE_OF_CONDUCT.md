# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn, especially for people making their very first
open-source contribution. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person. Explain *why* and suggest a fix.
- Accept feedback gracefully. A review request is not criticism of you.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions when something is unclear.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, including low-effort PRs made only to "count" a contribution
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a classroom or workplace

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and review comments) and at
workshops and events where the project is used.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately (through the
repository owner's GitHub profile, or tell a workshop organiser in person). Reports are handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or PRs, or block contributors who break this code of
conduct.
